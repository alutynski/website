Augusta Lutynski

 projects

Augusta has an ongoing practice in textiles and apprenticed as a shoemaker in 2015 in Krakow, Poland. She holds a degree in industrial design from Emily Carr University of Art and Design graduating in 2020 with a focus on narrative design and research. In 2025 Augusta graduated from The National Theatre School of Canada in scenography design.

Augusta is based in Vancouver BC. For inquiries or commissions contact: augustalutynski@gmail.com
